One bold insight

Speakers will share short, bold insights designed to challenge, inspire, and spark conversation as we close out 2026 and look ahead to a new year of AI progress.

We’re looking for one clear, memorable insight, something that can shift how people think as we enter 2027. This could be:

  • A fresh angle or unpopular opinion
  • Something you built, tested, learned, or changed your mind about
  • An AI development people are overlooking
  • A challenge or opportunity for Atlantic Canada
  • What AI is changing in your profession or community
  • A future we should intentionally create or intentionally avoid

The format

Selected speakers will deliver a focused 6–8 minute presentation built around one central message.

Talks should be accessible to both technical and non-technical audiences and grounded in real experience, evidence, research, or a specific example.

Who should apply

Everyone is welcome. If you have a bold insight, we want to give you the stage.

Researchers, professionals, founders, students, builders, educators, public servants, creators, and community members are all encouraged to apply. Previous speaking experience is not required.

Women, gender-diverse people, emerging speakers, and voices underrepresented in AI conversations are especially encouraged to apply.

How proposals are assessed

Proposals will be assessed on:

  • Clarity and strength of the central insight
  • Originality
  • Grounding in evidence or experience
  • Relevance to AI and Atlantic Canada
  • Accessibility
  • Contribution to a diverse program
